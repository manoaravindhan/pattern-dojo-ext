# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive community. By participating, you agree to:

- Be kind, patient, and assume positive intent
- Provide constructive, actionable feedback
- Welcome differing opinions without personal attacks
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and disruptive behavior

## Scope
Applies to all project spaces: issues, discussions, PRs, docs, and community chats.

## Reporting
If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please email the maintainers or open a confidential issue describing:
- What happened (with context)
- Where it occurred (links, screenshots if possible)
- Your contact (optional)

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may take action deemed appropriate, including warnings or removal from community spaces. Persistent or severe violations may lead to bans.

Thank you for helping keep this project welcoming for everyone.
